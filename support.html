02 — FAQ

Common Questions

How does XP work?

IronScape converts your workouts into experience points (XP). Different exercises contribute to different muscle skill categories — chest, back, legs, cardio, and more — so your skill profile reflects how you actually train.

Does IronScape require an internet connection?

Most functionality works offline — workout and nutrition data are stored locally on your device. An internet connection is required for account sync, social features, and challenges.

Will my progress be saved if I close the app?

Yes. All logged workouts, meals, and XP progression are saved locally and synced to your account, so you can resume anytime — even on a new device.

How do I report a bug?

Email us at support@ironscapefitness.com with the following details:

  • What happened and what you expected instead
  • Your iPhone model and iOS version
  • The IronScape app version (found in Settings)
  • Screenshots or a screen recording if possible

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account inside the app. This permanently removes all your data from our servers and cannot be undone.

04 — Troubleshooting

Quick Fixes

  1. Make sure the app is updated to the latest version in the App Store.
  2. If a screen appears stuck or unresponsive, force-quit the app and reopen it.
  3. If issues persist, restart your device and try again.
  4. Check your internet connection if social or sync features aren't working.
  5. If nothing resolves the issue, contact us at support@ironscapefitness.com with your device details.
