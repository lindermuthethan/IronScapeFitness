01 — Overview

Overview

IronScape Fitness ("IronScape", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile fitness application available on iOS (App Store bundle ID: com.lindermuth.ironscape). We built IronScape to help you track workouts, hit personal records, and connect with others who share your drive.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how we store and protect it, and the choices you have over your data. We've written it in plain language on purpose — privacy shouldn't require a law degree.

The short version

We collect only what's needed to run the app and serve you well. We do not sell your data. We do not use advertising networks or analytics SDKs. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time.

03 — How We Use Your Data

How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect strictly to operate and improve IronScape Fitness. Specifically:

  • To create and authenticate your account
  • To store and sync your workout, nutrition, and body data across devices
  • To calculate personalised calorie and macro recommendations based on your body metrics and goals
  • To display your profile, PRs, and workout history to followers (or publicly, depending on your settings)
  • To power social features — following, challenges, and community engagement
  • To allow other users to search for and follow you by username
  • To enable you to recover your data if you switch devices or reinstall the app
  • To investigate and fix technical issues if you contact us for support

We do not use your data to serve advertisements, build marketing profiles, or make automated decisions that significantly affect you.

04 — Third-Party Services

Third-Party Services

IronScape uses one third-party backend service:

Supabase — supabase.com

All user data described in this policy is stored on Supabase servers. Supabase provides our database, authentication system, and file storage (for profile photos). Supabase is GDPR compliant. You can review Supabase's own privacy practices at supabase.com/privacy.

We do not use any advertising SDKs, third-party analytics services, crash-reporting services, or social login providers beyond what is described above. If this changes in a future version of the app, this policy will be updated.

05 — Data Sharing

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data to anyone. Here is what is shared, and with whom:

  • With your followers: If your profile is public, or if a user follows you and you approve them on a private profile, they can see your workout logs, skills XP, personal records, and profile information (photo, bio, username).
  • With the IronScape community: Your username is searchable by any IronScape user. Display name, bio, and profile photo visibility are governed by your privacy setting.
  • Workout templates: If you enable the "copyable" toggle on a template, other users can copy it. This is off by default.
  • With Supabase: As our backend provider, Supabase processes and stores your data on our behalf under a data processing agreement. They are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes.
  • Legal obligations: We may disclose information if required to do so by law or to protect the rights, property, or safety of IronScape, our users, or others.
06 — Your Privacy Controls

Your Privacy Controls

IronScape gives you direct control over your privacy from within the app:

  • Public vs. Private profile: Set your profile to Public (visible to all IronScape users) or Private (only approved followers can see your data).
  • Follow approval: On a private profile, other users must send a follow request that you approve before they can view your content.
  • Copyable templates: Choose whether your workout templates can be copied by others. Disabled by default.
  • Sign out: You can sign out of your account at any time from within the app.
  • Delete your account: Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This permanently and irreversibly deletes all your data from Supabase, including your profile, workouts, nutrition logs, body metrics, and social data.
07 — Data Retention

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your IronScape account exists. We keep data only as long as needed to provide the service to you.

When you delete your account (via Settings → Account → Delete Account), all your personal data is permanently deleted from Supabase's servers. This action cannot be undone. If you want to preserve any workout history or records before deleting, we recommend exporting or noting down anything important first, as we do not currently offer a bulk data export tool.

Depending on where you live, you may have certain rights regarding your personal data. We respect these rights regardless of your location:

👁

All your data is viewable in-app. Your workout history, nutrition logs, profile, and settings are always accessible to you.

✏️

🗑

📬

CCPA / GDPR

We do not sell personal data. California and EU/EEA residents can exercise additional rights by contacting us directly.

To exercise any of these rights or if you have a privacy concern that can't be resolved in-app, please contact us at the email address below.

09 — Children's Privacy

Children's Privacy

IronScape Fitness is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us immediately and we will delete the account and all associated data.

If you are between 13 and 17, please ensure a parent or guardian has reviewed this policy before using the app.
