Core Features

Built for Lifters
Who Want More

IronScape combines structured workout logging with a dark RPG-inspired system, turning training consistency into visible progression.

⬆

Skill Progression

Train muscle groups and activities to earn XP and level up a profile that grows over time — like a character build based on your real effort.

⌁

Fatigue Tracking

Monitor training load and recovery so hard sessions feel rewarding without ignoring the realities of fatigue management.

◫

Workout Builder

Create, save, and reuse custom sessions while tracking sets, reps, weight, and effort with a clean mobile workflow.

◔

Nutrition Tracking

Log meals, calories, and macros so your training and nutrition systems live together in one cohesive app.

▣

Social Challenges

Create and join challenges, follow other lifters, and share progress. Private or public — you control your profile visibility.

↗

Progress Dashboard

See how skill levels, XP, and PRs evolve over time so every session contributes to a larger arc of visible progress.

What We Collect

Email address, display name, username, optional profile photo and bio, workout and nutrition data, body metrics, and social activity. All stored securely via Supabase.

How We Use It

Solely to run the app — syncing your data across devices, powering skill progression, nutrition tracking, and social features. We do not sell your data or use advertising SDKs.

Your Controls

Set your profile to Public or Private. Delete your account and all data at any time from Settings → Account → Delete Account.
